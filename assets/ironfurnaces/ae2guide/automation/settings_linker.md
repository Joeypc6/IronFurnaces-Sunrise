# Settings Linker

<ItemLink id="item_copy" /> copies one furnace's settings to others.

- Right-click a furnace: copies its sides, Auto Input and Output, redstone mode, Auto Split and facing.
- Sneak and right-click another furnace: pastes them; it turns to face the same way.
- Paste as often as you like.
