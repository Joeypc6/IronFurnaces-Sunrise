# The config tab

The tab at the furnace screen's top left opens its settings.

- **Auto Input**, **Auto Output**: the furnace moves items itself ([more](auto_io.md)).
- Six side buttons, Bottom to Right: what each face does ([more](sides.md)).
- Click a side for the next setting, right-click for the one before; Shift-click Front to reset all sides.
- Three redstone buttons: Ignored, Low, High ([more](redstone.md)).

Outside the tab: the augment button shows the [augment](../augments/index.md) slots, and a factory has the [Auto Split](../augments/auto_split.md) button.
