# Generator fuels

The red augment sets what a generator burns:

- None: furnace fuel, 20 RF per tick of burn time (coal 32,000 RF, lava bucket 400,000 RF).
- <ItemLink id="augment_smoking" />: food, 16,000 RF per hunger point (steak: 128,000 RF).
