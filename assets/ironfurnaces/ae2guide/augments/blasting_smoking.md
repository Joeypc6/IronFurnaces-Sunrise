# Blasting and Smoking

Red augments: the furnace takes only those recipes, which are twice as fast.

- <ItemLink id="augment_blasting" />: blasting recipes (ores, raw metals, armour).
- <ItemLink id="augment_smoking" />: smoking recipes (food).
- The furnace takes the blast furnace's or smoker's look.
- In a [generator](generator.md) they change the [fuel](../energy/fuels.md): metals and gems, or food.
