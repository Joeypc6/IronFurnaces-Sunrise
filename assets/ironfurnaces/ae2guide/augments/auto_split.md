# Auto Split

A button on the factory's screen: one input shared over every row.

- Put a stack in one row: the empty rows each get some.
- After each smelt the rows even out again, so they finish together.
- Off by default; the button's tooltip shows ON or OFF.
- Not on furnaces that smelt many at once (Rainbow, Allthemodium, Vibranium, Unobtainium).
