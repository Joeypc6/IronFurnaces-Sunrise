# Augments

Three slots, one per colour, one augment in each.

- **Red**: [Blasting or Smoking](blasting_smoking.md): which recipes.
- **Green**: [Speed or Fuel Efficiency](speed_fuel.md): time against fuel.
- **Blue**: [Factory](factory.md) or [Generator](generator.md): what the furnace is.

To fit one:

- right-click the furnace holding it (the old one pops out), or
- open the furnace and press the augment button for the slots.

Changing the blue augment drops the furnace's items (the augments stay); so does the red one on a generator.
