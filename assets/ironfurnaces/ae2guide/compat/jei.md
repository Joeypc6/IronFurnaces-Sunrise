# With JEI

With JEI installed, it lists what generators burn and the RF each gives:

- **Augment: Generator**: furnace fuels.
- **Generator | Blasting**: the [blasting fuels](../energy/fuels.md).
- **Generator | Smoking**: food.

The furnaces and the Factory augment show as smelting machines, the Blasting and Smoking augments for their recipes. W over an item in JEI opens its page here.
