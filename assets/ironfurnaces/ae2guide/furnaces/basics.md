# Using a furnace

- Top slot: what to smelt. Bottom slot: fuel. Right: the result.
- A fuel smelts as many items as in a vanilla furnace (coal: 8), however fast the furnace is.
- Experience waits inside until you take the results by hand, or break the furnace.
- A wet sponge with an empty bucket in the fuel slot fills the bucket.
- It lights up while it burns.
