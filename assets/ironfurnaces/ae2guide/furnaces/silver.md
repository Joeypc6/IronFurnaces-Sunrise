# Silver Furnace

- Smelts an item in **7 s** (140 ticks); a vanilla furnace takes 10 s.
- [Factory](../augments/factory.md): **4** rows.
- [Generator](../augments/generator.md): **100 RF/t**, buffer **200,000 RF**.
- Its recipes need silver ingots from another mod.
