# Upgrades

Change a furnace's tier where it stands.

- Sneak and right-click the furnace with the upgrade.
- An Iron Furnaces furnace keeps its items, energy, progress and settings.
- A vanilla furnace drops its items.
- With a Factory or Generator augment it drops its items (not the augments).
